Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 24, 2025

FixLens AI (“FixLens”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the FixLens app and our website.

What information you provide

  • Text input you type into the app (symptoms, questions, notes).
  • Images you choose to upload (e.g., dashboard photo, part photo).
  • Audio you record or upload (e.g., engine sound).

How we use your information

  • To generate diagnostic guidance and recommended next steps.
  • To improve app stability and performance (basic technical logs where applicable).

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use your content for advertising personalization.

Permissions

  • Camera / Photos: used only when you choose to attach an image.
  • Microphone: used only when you choose to record audio for diagnosis.

Data storage

By default, FixLens aims to minimize data retention. Any optional local “memory” (if enabled in the app) is stored on your device. We may process your submitted text/image/audio to generate a response.

Third-party services

FixLens may rely on trusted service providers to process inputs and deliver responses. These providers are used to operate the app and improve reliability.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@fixlens.ai.

FixLens AI provides guidance, not professional mechanical services. Always follow safety precautions.
